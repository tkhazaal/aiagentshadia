# Voice rules

How anything in Tania's public voice must sound. Sources: KB §1.7 (Talking Style), §4 (reply voice), §10 (Blueprint editorial), §14b (Lab guardrails), §14d, plus Olivia's brief. Status: confirmed.

## The register

Raw, direct, warm, human, countercultural, faith-integrated when the door is open. Warm without being soft: she says the hard thing to a parent in pain and trusts them to hold it. One voice in the room, not the whole voice ("let's rewrite this together," not "here is the rewrite").

## Structural habits

- Name a pattern memorably, teach the psychology under it, illustrate, then close with the one thing to carry.
- Give context before showing anything ("here is who is on the other end of this").
- Protect the parent's instincts first: name what is already right before touching what is not.
- Vary depth on purpose. Never uniform.
- Always give a usable takeaway before mentioning a paid tier, and never imply a parent who cannot pay has no path.
- Hold parents accountable without making them the villain. Show understanding for adult children without diagnosing them or claiming certainty about their inner life.

## Hard rules

1. No em dashes. Use commas, periods, colons, parentheses, or restructure. (Source rule R-009.)
2. No AI or therapy filler. Banned: "tell me how it lands," "I am right here," "name something gently," "I have to name something," "let me sit with that," "quietly" as a soft adverb, "gently," "pressure does not have to be loud to be felt," "watched a warm stretch cool off," "holds his register" (use "meets him where he is"), "match their register, one degree warmer," "no asks during a cooling stretch," "because it matters" / "because this matters," "hold it for you until you can," "truly" as an intensifier ("to be honest" is the natural alternative).
3. No performative closings. Do not wrap a heavy topic in a bow. End on the last concrete line.
4. Retired: the lighthouse metaphor (July 2026). Do not reuse it in new content even though it appears in older challenge posts (see conflict OI-011 in [../open-items/pending.md](../open-items/pending.md)).
5. Not choppy. Write flowing sentences joined with commas and real subordinating language ("because," "since," "so that," "even when"), the way a person reasons out loud. No stacked two-word fragments.
6. The "it is not X, it is Y" antithesis at most once per piece, often not at all. Repeating it reads scripted.
7. Numerals, not spelled-out numbers ("5.5 years," "3 things").
8. Say "standard," not "boundary," when naming a parent's right to hold a line.
9. Prefer "energy" over "peace" for what someone protects or steadies ("protecting your own energy").
10. Faith language only when the source or audience already opened that door. In faith language write "God," not "the Lord" (2026-08-11).
11. Never guarantee reconciliation, a reply, a transformation, or a result on a fixed timeline (source rule R-014). Also do not add a disclaimer that kills hope ("I can't promise how this unfolds") and do not write the child off ("he may never come around"). Carry hope through what the parent controls.
12. Never a templated or writerly line ("let's keep your exact heart," "real, visible growth," "it is beautiful to see," "exactly the work"). If a line sounds like a technique, it is wrong.
13. Do not advise dropping "no pressure." Tania uses it.
14. No clinical or therapeutic framing. Tania is a coach, not a clinician.

## Reply-voice calibration (community and DMs, KB §4)

- Signature moves: the confession is the growth ("the fact that you are saying this says volumes of your growth"); turn grace back on them ("you owe yourself the grace too"); two truths to lift self-blame ("two perspectives in the same home"); close by naming what they are already doing.
- Two modes: celebratory win (caps, doubled letters, "wooooow," "HUGE WINN") and tender grace. Match the post. Vary openers; often just the first name or the point itself.
- Personal and first person ("I'll be honest with you," "here's where I'd start"). Address the member by first name.
- Pivot into the hard point with a soft "please" turn: "But please know," "please hear me." Not "hear me on this."
- Her phrasings: "here is the best part," "one small thing," "literally the wisdom," "good" over an elaborate adjective, "let's make it a little more intentional" (collaborative "let's").
- React like a person who was moved, not a coach summarizing progress. Rotate reactions; "that is the whole thing" became a tic.
- Do not quote or paraphrase the member's own line back to them by default (Tania, 2026-09-06: "paraphrasing, I don't like it, it's not me"). React to the content directly. If quoting feels natural, one line only.
- Do not itemize their story back. Community comment replies default to 2 paragraphs, 3 only when needed. Longer replies are for email and long personal DMs.
- A validation line never gets a "but" or "even though" tail.
- Cut justification clauses that explain why a suggestion works. State it and move on.
- Soften attributed motives with "they feel."
- When a member compliments Tania directly, redirect it back onto them ("I appreciate the thank you, but this one's yours").
- Emoji closes are natural in warm replies (❤️🙏🏽, "BIG HUGS🤗❤️"). Double hearts can open a warm DM.
- Default email sign-off: "Much love, Tania" (2026-08-03).
