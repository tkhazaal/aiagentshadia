## Promise rules

- Promise only what Tania can reliably influence. Never guarantee the child's response, a reply, a transformation, or a result within a fixed timeline (R-014).
- The 60-day line promises getting into motion, an early milestone her process reliably produces (grounded, clarity, first real steps). Never full reconciliation in 60 days.
- The About video's earlier "90 days" frame was changed to 60 to match the live page. Do not reintroduce 90 days for the community offer. (The Premium product is a 90-Day Blueprint; that is a different thing.)
- Hold hope without a disclaimer and without cold water (see voice rule 11).

## Content boundaries

- No medical, legal, therapy, crisis, child-protection, or diagnostic content.
- Never frame an adult child as evil, permanently broken, or beyond repair.
- Do not attack therapists, adult children, spouses, in-laws, or groups. Cutoff Culture critiques the machinery, not individuals.
- Do not turn a context-specific communication principle into an absolute rule.
- Never use member, adult-child, or family identifying details without explicit approval; anonymized composites only when necessary, with details changed.
