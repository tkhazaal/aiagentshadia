# Support issue log

Structured record of complaints, cancellations, refunds, billing concerns, and access problems. Never expose one customer's details in another's reply. Status: no entries yet (2026-09-09).

Entry format:

```
## ISS-001: [short title]
Person:             (first name or initials only unless Shadia approves more)
Issue type:         complaint | cancellation | refund | billing | access | other
Product / order:
Date raised:
Communication history:  (dates, channel, summary)
Current status:     open | waiting on member | waiting on team | resolved
Owner:
Resolution:
Next action / due:
Source:             (DM, email, comment, meeting note)
Related:            (answer pattern, decision, link)
```

Standing policy reminders (see [../brand/approved-claims-and-guardrails.md](../brand/approved-claims-and-guardrails.md)):
- No refund guarantee exists as of 2026-09-03. Risk reversal is cancel-anytime only. Any refund request is a team decision, not something Olivia promises.
- Premium cadence is one Kickoff plus one 90-Day Reset call; requests for more 1:1s are redirected to chat, voice notes, and group calls.
