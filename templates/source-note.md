# Source note template

For every meaningful meeting, call, transcript, team discussion, DM thread, or email. File under `sources/` (verbatim material) or `communications/` (summaries) as `YYYY-MM-DD-type-slug.md`.

```
# [Title]

Date:
Type:            meeting | call | transcript | Slack | email | DM | comment thread | user instruction
Participants:
Source:          (where it came from, who supplied it)
Status:          confirmed | pending | needs verification

## What happened / what was learned
(reliable summary; keep exact wording where an instruction matters)

## Decisions
(each one also goes to decisions/decisions-log.md with a link back)

## Action items
| Item | Owner | Due | Status |

## Corrections, preferences, rules surfaced
(each one also goes to rules-and-preferences/ with a link back)

## Links mentioned
(each one also goes to links/link-registry.md)

## Related notes
```
