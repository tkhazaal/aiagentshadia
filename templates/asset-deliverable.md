# Asset deliverable template

Every finished asset Olivia returns uses this shape. Copy it into `content/drafts/YYYY-MM-DD-format-slug.md`.

```
Title:
Format and platform:
Audience segment:
Status: concept | draft | approved | published
Source:            (file or transcript name; "none, concept requiring Tania's approval" if absent)

Core angle:
Source grounding:  (the exact truth the source supports, with citation)
Publish-ready draft:
Production notes:
CTA:               (approved link only; otherwise "[confirm current link/claim]")
Approval check:    (answers to the seven questions below)
```

## Per-format deliverables

Reel, TikTok, or short video:
- 3 hook options
- Natural spoken script
- On-screen text
- Shot, B-roll, and editing notes
- Caption, CTA, optional pinned comment
- Best clip and timestamps when a transcript is supplied

Carousel:
- Slide-by-slide copy, normally 6 to 9 slides
- Strong opening slide
- Caption and simple visual direction
- One CTA slide

YouTube or podcast episode:
- 5 accurate title options
- Description
- Chapters when timestamps exist
- Thumbnail-text ideas
- Social clips and captions to repurpose

Podcast outreach:
- Personalized pitch (Tania as guest, or invitation to Tania's show)
- Verified reason the audience fit is strong
- 2 to 3 relevant topic angles
- Booking link only after confirmation, otherwise "[confirm current link/claim]"
- No claim that the recipient knows Tania or has agreed to anything

Content planning or audience-insight review:
- Themes grouped by audience need, platform, source, and CTA
- Repeated questions and comment themes, anonymized
- Observed evidence separated from creative hypotheses
- No invented metrics or results

## The final check (answer all seven before marking a draft ready)

1. Is every claim true and approved? (See [../brand/approved-claims-and-guardrails.md](../brand/approved-claims-and-guardrails.md).)
2. Does it offer hope without a guarantee?
3. Does it sound like Tania rather than generic AI or clinical advice? (See [../brand/voice-rules.md](../brand/voice-rules.md).)
4. Is the adult child portrayed with understanding, not blame or certainty?
5. Is there one clear takeaway and one CTA?
6. Does it protect private stories?
7. Did you remove em dashes, banned phrases, invented details, and stale offer claims?
