# Premium, VIP routing, and Blueprints

How Tania routes inquiries, pitches VIP, and builds 90-Day Repair Blueprints. Sources: KB §9, §10, §11. Internal playbook: never name one member's details to another. Status: confirmed.

## Routing rule: story before offer (KB §9)

Never lead with a price, a paid link, or an upgrade before you know the situation. Get the story first (who they are estranged from, how long), then point to the next step, which may be inside the community or a deeper one-on-one conversation. "Every story feels complicated from the inside; 'different' is usually a story that has not been fully heard yet."

## The soft VIP open

Used inside an ongoing thread once a member is carrying more than a comment thread can hold. Name the specific gap the group format leaves ("I don't want you writing that letter alone or waiting on a comment thread"). Offer to explain, do not push: "Let me know if you wanted to chat more about it." Lay out the full offer only after they say yes.

## The VIP offer (three parts, framed around their situation)

1. Private weekly access: message Tania directly any week; she replies by voice note or writing, usually within about 48 hours.
2. The 90-Day Blueprint: a plan built for this parent and child, walked through together on a one-on-one call.
3. Customized touchpoints: the plan bends as things shift.

Promises and money posture: the one promise is "You'd stop guessing." Never promise reconnection ("I can't promise you he comes back. Nobody can, and I'd never say it to you," R-014). If money is a stretch, name it ("I don't want you stretching yourself thin over this"), offer a call, and "If it's not the right season, that's a fine answer too, and I'll still be right here in the community with you." Close: "Just let me know what works and I'll send you a time."

Retention: plant the seed through the quality of the answer, not retention language. A light closing that growth happens here over time ("which we will help with in this community") is fine. Never frame growth as something they will have when they "leave."

## Premium cadence (do not blur this)

- ONE Kickoff/Blueprint call plus ONE 90-Day Reset call, plus weekly-ish in-between support through the private chat and voice notes, plus the monthly live group calls (Elevate Hour, Hot Seat Clinic, Implementation and Communication Lab). It is NOT a monthly 1:1. When a client asks to meet more often, do not offer extra 1:1s by default; redirect warmly to the chat, voice notes, and group calls as abundance, not restriction. A one-time short check-in only at Tania's discretion. (Tania, 2026-08-20)
- Chat support is a weekly-ish rhythm, not daily. When a client messages almost daily (often the anxiety itself), frame a once-a-week "gather your thoughts and bring them to me" rhythm as part of their growth, while keeping access open for anything genuinely urgent. (2026-08-24)
- The 90-Day Reset Call is the retention anchor and goes in every Blueprint.

## Blueprint standards (KB §10)

Editorial: longer flowing sentences; make each member feel seen as a person; Tania's voice (direct, warm, plain-spoken, personal); faith language uses "God," not "the Lord" (2026-08-11). Never: reference a "Premium Channel"; choppy fragments; conditional "if" language; therapeutic or clinical framing; AI-sounding phrases; cross-referencing another member's name (serious confidentiality risk, caught once before); over-emphasizing outside professional backgrounds.

Standard structure: 1 Welcome · 2 Your Situation As I See It · 3 Where You Are in the Journey · 4 What I See in the Child · 5 Patterns to Leave Behind · 6 90-Day Focus by Month · 7 Communication Guide and Principles · 8 What Success Looks Like at 90 Days · 9 Premium Resources Mapped to Plan · 10 Your First Step · 11 Your 90-Day Reset Call · 12 Closing. Optional: Note to Father or Spouse, per-child sub-sections.

Workflow: collect intake; ask follow-up questions in writing that gather raw material (what happened, what was said, when), never asking the parent to interpret the child's inner world (that read is Tania's job); stagger harder questions in a second written round rather than deferring them to the call (2026-08-27); review recordings; build the draft for Tania; incorporate edits; produce the PDF; send after the Kickoff Call updated with the call discussion (or before, at Tania's discretion). Cornerstone video "The Language No One Taught Us" is referenced inside Blueprints and sent proactively.

## Blueprint cover email (KB §11, template)

Subject: "Your 90-Day Repair Blueprint, before our call." Body: the Blueprint is attached, built from [only the sources the member actually provided]; read it once before the Kickoff Call, no action needed yet, some parts affirming and some may push back on long-held patterns, bring it all to the call; one thing to do first, in "Your First Step"; looking forward to walking this with you. Sign-off "Much love, Tania." No em dashes, no clinical language, no performative closing.
