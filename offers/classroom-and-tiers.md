## The 100+ Scripts & Prompts Bundle categories (KB §6)

1 First Gentle Reach-Outs · 2 Apology & Acknowledgment · 3 Re-Openers After Silence · 4 Holiday & Birthday · 5 Milestone Moments · 6 Neutral Everyday Check-Ins · 7 When They Reach Out First · 8 Repair Prompts (fill-in-the-blank) · 9 Boundary-Safe Messages · 10 Outside Influence (spouse, in-laws, third party) · 11 Adoptive / Identity Dynamics · 12 Mental Health / Personality Concerns · 13 Grandparents · 14 When They Reach Out First (short neutral reply) · 15 Fragile / Ongoing Contact. Plus: Tone & Timing Cheat Sheet, Golden Rules, Top 10 Mistakes to Avoid.

Member guidance as given: pick one script, keep it 1 to 3 sentences, send without expectation, one message at a time, stay consistent, safety first and closeness later. Closing note references God and points to the Repair Map and the Renewal Collective.

## Entry products (KB §1.6)

Reconnect Starter Kit, Cutoff Culture, the 100+ Scripts Bundle, the Repair Map, a Q&A vault. Prices are not in the source; do not state them.

## Tiers and the TIER RULE

| Tier | Includes | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Base community (the Renewal Collective) | Classroom, Q&A on the discussion board, Communication Lab (monthly live call and replays), monthly calls, daily replies from Tania, community | $89/mo, cancel anytime (KB §2) |
| Premium / VIP | Everything above plus Hot Seat Clinic, Elevate Hour, Implementation and Communication Lab call, private chat and voice notes, 90-Day Repair Blueprint, Kickoff and 90-Day Reset calls, Voice Memos, 48-Hour Hot Moments form. No separate Premium channel. | Not in source |
| 1:1 consultation | Paid, limited, positioned as a diagnostic rather than a coaching session (KB §1.6) | Not in source |

TIER RULE (repeat mistake, check every time): for a member who is not confirmed Premium, only route to the Q&A board, the Communication Lab, or the monthly calls. Never mention the Hot Seat Clinic to a non-Premium member. If unsure, default to the Q&A board only. (KB §4)

Note: the Elevate Hour is described in KB §13a as a premium call, while the base page promises "Monthly Live Lab + Replays." Treat Elevate Hour and Hot Seat as Premium-only when routing.
