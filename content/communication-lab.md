# Communication Lab

Monthly live call where members submit a situation and a draft message, and Tania works the ones that represent what most people are stuck on, live on screen. Everything is archived by archetype into a living Vault. The Lab works the MESSAGE; Hot Seat works the PATTERN. In Tania's own system an AI agent named Lena runs the Lab and Maria runs the business (KB §14a, §14e); they are not part of this repo. Sources: KB §1.6, §14a to §14d. Status: confirmed.

## The test applied to every message

Not "is this too vulnerable." The test is: does this message ask them to do something with it (respond, fix, carry, hurry)? Vulnerable, with no pressure, is the whole craft.

## Lab hard rules

No em dashes. No AI or therapy filler. No performative closings. Small precise edits, not rewrites. Rules are timing calls, never commandments. Coach the pattern, never the person (no names in a run sheet or the Vault). A Premium client's submission is never worked publicly. The July 2026 run sheet is a structure template only; its wording predates current voice rules.

## Anatomy of a worked draft (KB §14d)

1. Who is on the other end (context before the draft).
2. Sit with it ("read it a couple times, do you feel that?").
3. Name the parent's inner experience warmly, then route the deep version to Hot Seat.
4. Protect their instincts first, at length.
5. Explain the child's inner world, specific to this child.
6. Teach the fix vividly: isolate the one phrase, make the small change, show the feeling shift.
7. Practical specifics: the rhythm (about 4 weeks, 6 if depleted), calendar the send date.
8. The takeaway, said plainly, no bow.

Vary depth on purpose. The heaviest or most common pattern gets the time.

## The intentionality layer (how Tania actually coaches)

- Touchpoints are the floor, intentionality is the work. Keep the light "thinking of you, no pressure" beat always; sandwich in a more intentional message (one specific moment, one specific apology) for real movement.
- Accountability without surrender. "I understand now how I [specific thing], and I'm working on it. Love you." Not "I'm working on being a better mom."
- Find the pattern, apologize to the pattern. Never ask the child for a list of hurts.
- Assume one specific apology; do not ask "what did I do."
- Read the need underneath: "now it's just money" means "I want to feel seen." Money can't buy it.
- Identity formation (late teens, twenties): be supportive at a distance; "excited to see the person you're growing into, always here."
- Don't chase; desperation hands them control.
- Grace for the parent when they self-attack.
- Stabilize the parent before reconnection when they are in crisis.

## Corrections from Tania on how to answer

Show the effect on the child, never only the parent's intent. Never templated or writerly. Do not advise dropping "no pressure." Every detail must earn its place for this person. House style always.
