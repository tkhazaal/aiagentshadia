# Hot Seat Clinic pattern library

The inner patterns Hot Seat teaches, the ones a parent recognizes in themselves. Premium-only material; never mention Hot Seat to a non-Premium member. Convention: name, the inner story, the tell, the work. Empty Tank and the Silence Loop are the stable signature frames; the rest rotate names to keep teaching fresh. Source: KB §12. Status: confirmed.

## Community patterns (recur across the base)

- Grandkids as the Gate: pushing for access lands as a threat. Keep grandkid touches light, thank the adult child without heaviness, never go around them.
- The Story Someone Else Is Telling: an ex, partner, or aligned therapist authors the "toxic" narrative. Do not fight the author; outlast the story.
- The Partner or In-Law Wedge: a condition like "accept my partner or nothing." Do not argue the terms; keep the direct line warm; drop the campaign. Biggest community signal Aug 2026 (about 11 of 20 quiz-takers).
- When the Wedding Doesn't Come: excluded from a milestone. Do not campaign for a seat, do not disappear; one warm no-ask signal; carry the grief sideways.
- The Blocked Wall: blocked everywhere. A block is a boundary, not a verdict; never go around it; hold the door from your side; build your own life.

## The healthy model

- The Clean Own: naming one specific ownable thing with no defense near it, an open door with no clock. Where the inner work is headed.
