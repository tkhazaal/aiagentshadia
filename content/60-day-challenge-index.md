# The 60-Day Estranged Parent Challenge: day index

Index of every daily post in KB §7, with each day's closing line (Tania's own words, hook-length, repurposable). Full posts are in the source. Format of every post: title, short teaching, "Today's movement," "Check in below," closing line. Days marked ★ are Weekly Table days. Status: confirmed as source content; runs on a schedule (dates need verification, OI-008).

Reuse notes: closing lines are in Tania's voice and can seed hooks. Days 38 and 50 carry personal-story details beyond the one approved fact (see OI-012). Week 7 is titled "The Lighthouse" and Days 39 and 59 use the light metaphor, which Tania retired in July 2026 (OI-011). Day 32 contains a placeholder "[paste Communication Lab replay link]". Day 41's closing line says "the rhythm that brings your child home"; do not lift that phrase into new public content because it edges toward a promise.
